About Event Multipliers

An event multiplier is your manual estimate of how much more (or less) demand you expect on a special day. Example: a multiplier of 1.5 means you expect ~50% more demand than a normal day. This is not an AI/ML prediction — it is your experience-based estimate that will guide preparation planning in Sprint 5.

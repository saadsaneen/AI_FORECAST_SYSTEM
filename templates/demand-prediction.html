Model Selection & Transparency Notice

Evaluation-Guided Model Assignment: In our historical model evaluation, Pathiri achieved lower Mean Absolute Error (MAE) using Model 1 (Calendar Features). Conversely, Appam, Chappathi, Porotta, and Biriyani achieved lower MAE using Model 2 (Calendar + Previous 3 Recorded Sales). Therefore, the system automatically assigns Model 1 for Pathiri, and Model 2 for the remaining dishes.

Graceful Fallback Handling: When fewer than three prior sales observations exist for a dish prior to the target date, the system transparently falls back to Model 1 without substituting artificial zeros.

⚠️ Machine learning demand forecasts are statistical estimates based on historical patterns and should be combined with kitchen staff experience and real-time footfall conditions.
